# Photograph provenance and licenses

The photographs are bundled for offline educational catalog initialization. Product details, prices and pet names are fictional training data. The two Photos Public Domain assets use 1024-pixel versions from the WordPress image CDN. The application produces resized JPEGs and thumbnails without EXIF through its normal image service. Adapted dog-food images remain licensed CC BY-SA 2.0; attribution is included in the public card and media metadata. Other photographs retain their CC0/public-domain status.
